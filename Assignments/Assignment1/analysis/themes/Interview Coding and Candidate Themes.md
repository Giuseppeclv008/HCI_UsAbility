# Interview Coding – Full Remote Work Under 35

## Coding

To analyze the data collected during the needfinding interviews, we adopted a thematic analysis approach.

We examined the interview transcripts to identify meaningful statements related to the participants’ experiences of fully remote work, including their daily routines, work-life boundaries, social relationships, working environments, and well-being.

Relevant excerpts were assigned descriptive codes representing specific experiences, behaviors, perceptions, or challenges. When appropriate, multiple codes were assigned to the same excerpt to capture different aspects of the participant’s experience.

The following tables present the identified codes, their descriptions, and the interviews in which they appear.

* Ui with i=1,…,4 → Interview to user i;
* EU → interview to the extreme user;
* PSI → interview to the psychologist (domain expert)

* **EU (C17, C20):** Prefers flexible work-life boundaries, unlike the psychologist, who highlights potential risks.
