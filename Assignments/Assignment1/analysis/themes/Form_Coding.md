# Form Coding - Full Remote Work Under 35

## Coding

To analyze the data collected through the needfinding questionnaire, we adopted a thematic analysis approach.

We examined the open-ended responses of 15 participants to identify meaningful statements related to their experiences of fully remote work, including daily routines, work-life boundaries, social relationships, flexibility, and well-being.

A total of 158 excerpts were analyzed and assigned 56 descriptive codes representing specific experiences, behaviors, perceptions, or challenges. When appropriate, multiple codes were assigned to the same excerpt to capture different aspects of the respondent’s experience.

The coding was primarily semantic and inductive, focusing on the participants’ expressed experiences rather than predefined categories. Closed-ended responses were not coded.

## Questionnaire Data Structure and Source References

The questionnaire responses were collected in a spreadsheet, where each row represents a respondent and each column corresponds to a specific question.

Respondents are identified by anonymous IDs based on their spreadsheet row (e.g., R2). Each excerpt is linked to its original spreadsheet cell, where **the letter identifies the question and the number identifies the respondent’s row**. For example, K2 refers to the response provided by respondent R2 to the question in column K.

## Reviewing Candidate Themes

The codes identified from the questionnaire responses were used to review and refine the six candidate themes previously developed from the interviews.

Rather than creating a separate set of themes for the questionnaire, we compared the two sets of codes (C01–C66 from the interviews and S01–S56 from the questionnaire) to identify recurring patterns, additional perspectives, and contrasting experiences.

This comparison informed the subsequent thematic review, during which the initial themes were retained, refined, split, or reorganized to develop the final thematic structure.

The review process and the resulting final themes are documented separately in *Theme Review and Final Themes*.
