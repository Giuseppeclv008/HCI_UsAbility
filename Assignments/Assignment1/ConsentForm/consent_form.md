## 2. What Participation Involves
* **Duration:** The interview will take approximately **30 to 60 minutes**.

* **Artifacts & Photos:** We may request to take photographs of relevant physical/digital artifacts or work environments. **No identifiable photos of your face will be taken** without separate, explicit consent.

* **Data Security & Retention:** Audio recordings, transcripts, and notes will be stored securely and will be deleted once the academic evaluation is completed.
